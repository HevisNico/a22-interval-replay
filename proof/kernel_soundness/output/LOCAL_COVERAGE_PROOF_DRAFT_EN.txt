1. What must actually be proved
------------------------------

Fix an admissible physical parameter vector theta and initial physical
state x0. Let a physical trajectory include its ordered emission history,
not merely its terminal charges. A covering local computation must supply
a consistent representation of that SAME trajectory throughout the cell.

The desired assertion is:

  For every admissible theta, x0 and represented parent input history,
  if the cell returns a complete result, that result covers the complete
  physical output history and physical terminal state at time 1116.

The quantifiers are universal over physical realizations and existential
over their abstraction witnesses. They do not say that every abstract
branch is physically realizable. Overapproximation and spurious branches
are permitted; losing one real branch is not.

The important induction points are unit-loop heads and accepted macro
returns. Requiring every temporary object produced by flowing() to be a
physical state would be incorrect: the implementation deliberately uses
counterfactual READY and continued-protection calculations.

2. A relational representation, Gamma
------------------------------------

Write Gamma(theta, trajectory, S, P, O, eta) for the following assertions.
S is a stored cell state, P its remaining exterior queue, O its completed
output prefix, and eta one consistent assignment of affine noise values.

G1 — Time and symbols. Each attested time atom denotes the same physical
quantity at every use of its identifier. Its stored affine definition is
immutable. A time's absolute box and its expression both contain its
physical date. Existing noise assignments are preserved; genuinely fresh
abstraction symbols may be assigned new values when they are introduced.
The physical parameters retain their selected theta values throughout.

G2 — Physical endpoint. At the selected physical control time, each live
charge, log-gain and recovery rate is enclosed by the corresponding state
representation. Mode flags are the physical modes. READY loop states must
be below the firing threshold after all due transitions are processed;
FAST and SLOW have their physical elapsed-age or log-charge constraints.
Energy is covered by the previously proved [50,100] barrier.

G3 — Raw and auxiliary origins. An active raw flag identifies one actual
parent pulse, its immutable origin and its end origin+1. The active MARK
or ERROR origin belongs to the current auxiliary cycle on that port.
Origins left in inactive fields are not interpreted as active currents.
At READY-memory creation every raw-tail age is physically in [0,1].

G4 — Histories. For each actual gated MARK/ERROR history there is a retained
ledger representing its baseline, completed transfers, active segment,
port parameter, and actual gate endpoints. Different ledgers may be
combined into a larger product set, provided the actual combination is
still present with the same time/noise assignment. For protected charge,
the selected actuation history represents the integral of that same b.

G5 — READY memory. When memory is present, its anchor denotes the actual
READY start, its stored q encloses charge there, and its pulse terms use
the actual gains at admission. For every relevant counterfactual READY
continuation, evaluating this memory encloses its charge. A missing
memory is allowed only with a sound scalar state and scalar flow formula.

G6 — Pending work. P contains every unprocessed exterior action generated
by the complete parent histories. Every processed action occurs in the
covered prefix or accepted macro. An overlap-window endpoint cannot skip
a pending action lying strictly inside that window.

G7 — Emissions. O covers the entire emission prefix through the control
point, with shared date identities preserved. A join cannot equate an
optional extra pulse with no pulse. Conditional histories with distinct
emission sequences remain separate or cause a refusal.

G8 — Reachable joins. A retained first branch's discrete fields and memory
origins must represent all joined branches. Equal token strings alone do
not establish this assertion; the construction of those objects matters.

For a single physical trajectory, eta need not make every spurious branch
valid. It must select a covering path and remain consistent on that path.

3. The four kinds of intermediate state
--------------------------------------

L: a live state satisfying Gamma at a macro boundary.
R: a counterfactual READY continuation, possibly extended past its first
   threshold, used only to locate that threshold or bound its absence.
P: a continued-protection ledger, possibly held in a convenient temporary
   phase, used to accumulate marks and exclude reopening.
C: a conditional branch or root probe, meaningful only under its proposed
   weak order, phase and first-guard conditions.

These categories are proof predicates, not new runtime fields. They
explain why a temporary q value need not be an actual physical charge.
Conversion R/P/C -> L requires a proved guard, no-guard condition, or
covering branch argument. A final interval overlap alone is insufficient.

Example: protected_section_cluster keeps the FAST flag while processing
a window that can extend past F+1. Its temporary FAST charge is not the
physical SLOW charge there. This does not invalidate the macro if the
ledger/gates are correct and its final ell>0 excludes an earlier return.
Only then may it normalize q=R*exp(ell), phase=SLOW at the common endpoint.

4. Local analytic lemmas
-----------------------

L1 — Frozen gain during its own raw pulse.
The previously certified auxiliary inequalities place ERROR after raw
end, complete each auxiliary cycle before age 1.3, and separate same-port
inputs by more than 1.3. Therefore the gain on port j cannot change during
that port's current raw pulse. Changes on other ports do not alter it.
The amplitude stored on admission is consequently the correct amplitude
for the whole admitted part of that pulse.

L2 — READY memory is variation of constants.
For a new pulse at a, its contribution is g*h(t-a) for 0<=t-a<=1 and
g*h(1)*exp(-K*(t-a-1)) afterwards, where

  h(u)=A/(A-K)*(exp(-K*u)-exp(-A*u)).

For a pulse already active at a READY anchor tau, let v=tau-a and u=t-tau.
Its remaining contribution is

  g*exp(-A*v)*h(min(u,1-v))*exp(-K*max(u-(1-v),0)).

Add the homogeneous part R+(q(tau)-R)*exp(-K*u). These expressions follow
by integrating the linear READY equation on the admission interval and
then propagating the resulting charge by homogeneous decay. They justify
the memory evaluator under G3/G5, including raw cuts. Its derivative is
-K times the pulse contribution plus the raw current while admission is
active. The [0,1] age intersection is justified by G3, not by numerical
overlap of arbitrary age intervals.

L3 — First crossing, not just a root.
On a segment with no new raw arrival, the total current is a nonnegative
exponential between raw cuts. If d=q', then d'=-K*d-A*J. At d=0 with J>0,
the derivative of d is negative. A cut only decreases d. Thus d cannot
switch from negative to positive before another arrival. Starting below
1, an ascending root certified in this segment is its first crossing.
If J=0, relaxation toward R<1 cannot create a threshold crossing.
Tangency or an undecided sign does not satisfy a positive-slope root test.

L4 — Protected uniqueness.
After FAST, ell(t)=ln(2.5)-integral_(F+1)^t b(s) ds. Since b is positive,
ell is strictly decreasing and has at most one zero. A root bracket with
opposite weak signs and a positive derivative for -ell encloses that
unique return. The constant-rate shortcut requires cap saturation by F+1.

L5 — Completed transfers are sufficient statistics.
Integrating a drain of charge x -> y gives the actuator increment times
(x-y). A full MARK contributes .1 to b and a full ERROR contributes .6 to
z. A partial episode retains its actual gate endpoints. Completed terms
enter all future expressions only by addition to a baseline. Folding
those terms into a baseline enclosure preserves all future evaluations;
it does not authorize folding an active segment as if it were complete.

L6 — Saturation is a projection of a monotone potential.
For nonnegative admitted transfers, b=min(B,potential), and similarly for
z at its cap. For a cap-contact bracket [l,u], integration up to a trial
y0 in that bracket followed by the cap rate overestimates the true dose
by at most (u-l)*max(|raw_rate(l)-B|,|raw_rate(u)-B|). Subtracting the
interval [0,error] therefore encloses the capped integral. The optimized
formula requires contact to be certain and already before the minimum
evaluated endpoint; otherwise the monotone quadrature path is needed.

L7 — Union of affine forms is pointwise.
For any common coefficients a*_k, branch i differs from sum a*_k eta_k by
at most e_i+sum|a_ik-a*_k| plus its center interval. Taking the enclosing
center union preserves each branch at its original eta. Intersections
with a second enclosure are valid only for the same physical quantity.
These are algebraic lemmas; execution additionally relies on the audited
directed arithmetic and native implementation contracts.

5. Completeness of order enumeration
----------------------------------

Fix a physical weak order of the pending exterior actions. At every
recursive enumeration prefix, choose its next physical event. It cannot
be excluded by a strictly earlier remaining event test, a negative upper
bound for its elapsed time, or an impossible absolute interval order,
because the actual dates witness consistency. Its port automaton stage
is admissible by G3. Induction on the number of remaining events retains
at least one permutation for this physical weak order. Equality is not a
strict contradiction. If a resource bound interrupts enumeration, the
cell does not return a complete successful result.

This lemma concerns admissible_orders, not the separate ordinary advance
candidate-selection test discussed in Section 10.

For groups, the suffix-minimum construction examines all unprocessed
lower bounds. It expands the endpoint until no omitted event can lie
strictly inside the covered window. Events exactly at the endpoint may
be handled subsequently with zero elapsed time, provided due internal
guards have not been lost. That proviso is part of G2/G6.

6. Macro contracts and covering witnesses
----------------------------------------

M1 — Ordinary external cluster.
Entry: Gamma and a complete exterior group; advance to its lower bound
must itself be sound. For the actual order, each guardfree flow verifies
absence of an internal transition before applying the next action. Other
orders may be spurious but cannot replace this one. All paths reach the
same common endpoint with compatible modes and raw identities. Union of
their histories and q enclosures then covers the physical path. READY
memory joins require the stronger anchor property in Section 8.

M2 — FAST/SLOW protected cluster.
Entry: FAST with common fire F; the endpoint is after every possible F+1.
The FAST and SLOW gates agree: READY admission and gain actuation are
closed, while protected MARK actuation is open. Follow the actual weak
exterior order with these gates and accumulate all marks. Suppose a first
physical return occurred before the endpoint. Up to that return the
continued protected ledger equals the physical ledger, so ell is zero
there. Its continued b is positive, so ell cannot be positive later.
This contradicts the accepted endpoint lower bound ell>0. Thus no such
return occurred. Normalize the final charge and mode to the SLOW formula.
This first-exit argument, not the temporary FAST q, justifies the macro.

M3 — Recovery with dynamically independent auxiliary events.
Entry: SLOW; the independently predicted return stays valid under all
included actions. The caller excludes raw changes and new unsaturated
marks that would change this return. Insert the shared return into every
admissible weak order. The actual order survives; reopen applies the
physical READY section and relevant ERROR gates. Subsequent guardfree
flows and the final strict q<1 check exclude an extra emission. Branches
are joined at a common endpoint. At equality finite actuator currents
have zero integral over a zero-duration gate interval.

M4 — Legacy local-cycle resolver.
Entry: SLOW and a complete window. The certified width is <1 after the
earliest possible reopening, so at most one new fire is possible and its
FAST phase covers the rest of the window. For each possible exterior/
return order, recompute the return from that order's marks. Reject only
strict contradictions or disjoint physical phase domains. The effective
READY helper is garde_relative_A22.ready_to, installed through
ready_general_A22; the older absolute-time helper is not the target.

For the actual no-fire continuation the [0,1] endpoint branch is retained.
For an actual fire, scalar comparisons retain its elapsed-time range;
the closed subdivision cover cannot discard its true root because its
charge equals 1. Keeping the original elapsed anchor yields a covering
emission date. Following that branch to the endpoint gives its FAST
state. A global interval overapproximation may retain additional roots.

The single-train caller joins only a nonempty forest all of whose branches
are READY and have no new emissions. Otherwise it refuses or raises
BranchingRequired. Hence it cannot silently flatten an actual optional
emission. This argument assumes correct flow/cover evaluation and the
stronger join preconditions; it is not inferred from a zero profile count.

M5 — Grouped recovery fallback.
This calls the same local resolver and accepts the same zero-emission,
READY-only condition. No successful use was recorded in this campaign.
This does not imply zero uses of M4, which has a separate caller path.

M6 — Recorded fire/exterior commutations.
All 202 recorded successful uses have exactly one mark_off, two branches,
and zero fire-time widening. In READY the charge trajectory does not
depend on mark_off, so its first fire F is the same on both sides of that
event. If the mark ends first, none of it remains at F. If F is first,
the remaining MARK is integrated under protection. If the dates coincide,
the remaining transfer has zero duration. The true order is one of the
two computed prefixes, and the common endpoint is after both events and
strictly before F+1 and the next exterior event. The joined state is FAST
with one common fire object and no READY memory. This establishes the
mathematical specialization needed here under the root/flow contracts;
it does not use the broader raw-arrival lemma as an unproved shortcut.

M7 — Ordinary advance and no-fire fallback.
Scalar/memory strict maxima below 1 justify no fire, and a strictly
subthreshold closed cover also justifies no fire. A positive value of
the counterfactual guard at the exterior date proves an earlier first
crossing by continuity and L3/L4. Other accepted candidate selections use
weak relative-time comparisons. Their strict physical interpretation is
the outstanding implementation obligation in Section 10.

7. Shared inputs and layer induction
-----------------------------------

The local theorem must preserve a parent emission's identity at all its
destinations. Loading the same authenticated parent payload does that at
the data level; a globally consistent physical witness does it at the
semantic level. Local fresh symbols must not collide across producers.
The passive saved-data audit found zero inconsistent definitions among
63,888 distinct retained time atoms; it does not inspect discarded probes.

Assuming Gamma is initialized and preserved by all successful macros,
induction over the finite loop yields the complete emission train and
terminal inclusion for each cell. Induction over the 18 feedforward
layers then covers all 216 physical terminal states jointly. This is the
missing H_entry implication. The previously certified return inclusion
P_h(Q) subset Q for h>=20 then supplies future invariance and the stated
fixed-realization word preservation. Successful runs alone do not replace
the assumptions in this induction.

8. Why memory joins need a reachable-state lemma
----------------------------------------------

dep.join compares anchor tokens, pulse keys and tail flags. It retains the
first anchor and each first pulse-origin object, while hulling q and g.
A time token records the expression but not a possibly restricted absolute
box. Therefore arbitrary same-token memories are NOT a sufficient domain
on which dep.join is a sound union operation.

The sufficient condition is: the retained anchor's absolute box and its
expression cover that anchor for every joined physical branch, and every
retained pulse origin has the same property. Then L2 and the pointwise
q/g hull cover each branch. If anchors or term structure differ and memory
is dropped, the scalar state remains available; scalar continuation needs
the no-arrival segment condition and L1.

The effective call sites give the following route to this condition:

* Ordinary exterior joins preserve one common incoming memory anchor.
  New pulse origins come from the immutable common exterior queue.
* Recovery/auxiliary joins use one common precomputed return object and
  one common active-raw set to create all new memories.
* FAST/SLOW and recorded fire commutation joins have memory=None.
* Local-cycle joins recompute a return for each proposed order. Each
  nonpoint return duration receives a fresh time atom, so distinct return
  anchors fail the token-equality test and memory is dropped. A one-branch
  join retains only its own valid memory. Absolute restriction preserves
  the return expression but cannot unify distinct fresh atoms.

The last item needs a nonpoint-duration fact for return_with_marks.
Its guard contains an explicitly outward ln(2.5) interval. A promising
proof is to show strict enclosure at each exact zero, then preserve an
interior zero through interval Newton. This would exclude collapse to
an exact duration and therefore force temporal.shift's fresh-atom path.
This draft does not substitute a numerical observation of positive widths
for that all-reachable-calls proof. This join obligation and the ordinary
guard-order obligation share the strict-enclosure issue below.

Inactive origin fields do not cause an additional free choice: same-port
cycles do not overlap, and their current active origins come from the
same completed subset of the immutable queue. Discarded conditional labels
can enlarge the represented set, but cannot justify keeping an anchor box
that excludes one actual branch.

9. Two exact checks that rule out inadequate arguments
------------------------------------------------------

check_contract_countermodels.py uses rational arithmetic and alternating
Taylor bounds. It imports no A22 code. Its output is a negative test of
proof contracts, not a counterexample to the recorded run.

C1 — Same time token is insufficient.
Let atom A range over [0,2]. Two conditional branches restrict the same
anchor to [0,1] and [1,2]. Both have q(anchor)=.5, R=.1, K=.5 and no pulses.
At t=3 the first anchor box yields q <= .1+.4*exp(-1) < .25. The second
branch with A=2 has q=.1+.4*exp(-.5) > .30. Keeping only the first anchor
therefore excludes a physical value by more than .05, despite identical
tokens. Reachable call-site conditions, not token equality, must rule out
this configuration in A22.

C2 — A closed root enclosure plus weak order is insufficient.
Consider a separate minimal hybrid system x(0)=0, x'=1 until an exterior
cut at t=1, and x'=0 afterwards. Reaching x=1 emits a pulse and resets.
The exact first guard is at t=1. A sound closed root interval [1,2] permits
the exterior-first comparison inf(root-1)=0. Processing only the cut and
then searching for a future positive-slope root loses the emission.

This toy model does not show that A22 creates such a root interval or
accepts such a state. It shows why ordinary enclosure soundness cannot
alone complete the equality case. A22 needs either a stronger strict-root
property or explicit preservation of the due guard at equality.

10. The precise strict-root obligation
-------------------------------------

In effective advance_inner the candidate list starts with the exterior
boundary, then possibly includes a shifted root. It accepts an ordering
when each other candidate minus the selected one has lower bound >=0.
The source has a special equal-date FAST/SLOW wrapper; that alone does not
settle the READY fire case. act(raw_off) can remove the current, so one
cannot simply postpone an exactly due fire without proving its retention.

A sufficient lemma would be:

  U: For every actual represented first root r returned on an ordinary
  guard path, with the existing physical/noise witness fixed, the returned
  duration has a representation with strict residual slack that survives
  both forms of time subtraction used by Time.minus. Its newly allocated
  duration atom is absent from the pre-existing exterior queue. Therefore
  an accepted weak inequality against that root implies a strict physical
  inequality, unless a separately handled exact identity applies.

One must also handle the plain scalar ready_root route, affine_refine's
choice between correlated and plain forms, and roots formed from several
actuation histories. A positive marginal width is NOT equivalent to U.
An interval may have positive width and still contain a physical root at
its boundary for a particular parameter assignment.

A plausible proof decomposition is:

U1. Establish a pointwise interior-enclosure property for each actual
    guard evaluation at a root. For SLOW, outward ln(2.5) supplies a
    candidate source of strict slack. For READY, a real crossing from
    q<1 needs a nonzero admitted pulse response; exponential response
    bounds are candidate sources of strict slack.
U2. Prove that this slack survives all operations in the particular guard
    expressions, including multiplication by interval/affine gains,
    lattice branches, pulse tails, history hulls, and fresh-error promotion.
    Bounds on center intervals, not just final marginal widths, matter.
U3. Use endpoint signs and positive slopes to put actual roots strictly
    inside the initial bracket. Show interval Newton intersections and
    every allowed affine_refine return retain the required strict witness.
U4. Prove freshness and the corresponding strictness of both the absolute
    box subtraction and atom-expression subtraction. Only then may choosing
    the narrower result preserve the strict-order inference.

The elementary interval-Newton containment argument is available, and
the fresh-atom constructor is explicit. The full pointwise strictness
chain U1-U4 for all effective paths has NOT been established here. It is
not acceptable to claim that outward rounding "usually leaves a margin"
or that equality has probability zero: the theorem quantifies over every
admissible parameter, including boundary realizations.

An alternative execution-specific discharge would certify, for every
ordinary external advance of the authenticated run, strict no-fire up to
the CLOSED endpoint or a strict internal/exterior separation, together
with safe memory-join anchor data. The saved terminal DAGs and truncated
width profiles do not contain all such local predicates. The four H1/H2
reports do not certify every transition of all 216 cells. No new run or
replay was performed to try to manufacture the missing evidence.

11. What this deliverable establishes and does not establish
-----------------------------------------------------------

New concrete results are: a proposed global-witness relation Gamma;
separate semantics for live and counterfactual states; analytic proofs
for pulse memory, protected first-exit normalization, completed-history
folding and order coverage; macro-by-macro covering arguments with stated
premises; the necessary stronger memory-join contract; an explicit
strict-root proof target; and two exact countermodels showing why weaker
contracts cannot close that target.

The effective source views reconstruct 17 literal substitutions into eight
functions without importing or executing the original engine. They are
audit views, not a replacement executable. Runtime adapters not expressed
as substitutions remain part of the target and are listed in the audit.

The previously certified future closure remains conditional on H_entry.
This work narrows and formalizes the local soundness problem; it does not
discharge it. It neither proves that the campaign is wrong nor establishes
unconditional invariance for every admissible training trajectory. No
source patch, fresh campaign, or rerun is recommended merely because this
proof obligation is still open.
