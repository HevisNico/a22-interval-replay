A22 · FROZEN-NETWORK SELECTIVITY EXPERIMENT

What does the learned gain structure buy?

Six frozen configurations, seven input scenarios, three noise levels with twenty paired seeds each, plus one quiet example. Physical equations and costs are unchanged.

Two questions are evaluated jointly. Does a probe reach the output, and are true source pulses still recovered in its presence? Rejecting probes by failing to relay the true source is not a selective advantage. The homogeneous median rule is supplemented by a rule applied only after the learned entrance, so an entrance-broadcast failure cannot be mistaken for evidence of sophisticated interior computation.

Findings

The learned configuration has a real filtering effect relative to saturation. For isolated full-amplitude lateral probes, the fraction reaching layer 130 is 9.79%, 4.38% and 6.04% at low, medium and high noise, versus 100% for both ungated-trained and uniform4 networks. Strict true recall is 99.323%, 99.876% and 99.863%; inclusive true recall is 99.336%, 99.945% and 99.945%. This is strong rejection of probes, but not exact equality of true-signal reliability.

The homogeneous median rule is not an adequate broadcast baseline on its own. Its weak lateral gains prevent the single-lane source from populating all 12 required output lanes. That is why the additional control retains the learned entrance before applying the simple rule in layers 7–130. This hybrid retains all learned b values as well and should not be described as a fully hand-designed replacement for learning.

The learned network does not generally outperform that stronger hybrid control. The hybrid gives zero false-only transmissions for the tested isolated lateral probes, while recovering all true signals. It also handles weakened central probes very well. These results do not support a universal claim that fine-grained learned gains are necessary for selectivity.

Mixed background noise exposes a context-dependent tradeoff. At low and medium noise, learned inclusive true recall is 99.408% and 99.961%, versus 98.438% and 99.297% for the hybrid. Paired95% intervals for these differences are [+0.111,+1.986] and [+0.241,+1.149] percentage points. However, learned produces on average 13.8 and 11.8 false-only output events per trial, versus zero for the hybrid. The hybrid tends to merge probe provenance into true outputs, while learned lets fewer probe identities through but sometimes produces separate false emissions. The two measures must not be conflated.

Noise-only tests also depend on what is counted. At low and medium noise, fewer injected probe identities reach the output with learned than with the hybrid, but successful learned false probes spread to more output lanes and create more false output events. At high noise the hybrid is better on both probe reach and false-event count. There is no single global winner across these endpoints and contamination regimes.

The energy picture changes when contamination is included. For full-amplitude lateral probes, learned total modeled work is 12.88%, 13.98% and 13.92% lower than uniform4 with the same b profile. In clean tests learned had slightly higher work. Thus filtering can offset the earlier clean-use overhead by preventing downstream processing of probes. These are total costs at the observed, not exactly equal, true-signal recall; they are model units, not measured joules.

The result supports functional selectivity of the learned configuration relative to saturation, with competitive simple alternatives and context-dependent compromises. Directional bias was already present in the initial gains (0.3,1.2,0.3), so this experiment does not demonstrate discovering direction from isotropic initialization. All 2562 datasets passed the raw-event and energy audits,183 clean historical regressions matched, and no injection-timing or solver-domain failures were hidden or discarded.

The median pair was fixed from the 60 noisy normal-training checkpoints before probe results were inspected. It is a specified simple control, not a proven optimal static alternative. All tests freeze both gains and b, relax q to 0.1 and start E at 100. The ungated comparator changes both learned gain and recovery profiles; the uniform and median controls match b exactly to isolate gain-structure differences.

Directionality was not learned from an isotropic initialization. Nominal initial gains are (0.3,1.2,0.3): the central port already starts four times stronger. The experiment tests adaptation and calibration of a biased architecture, not discovery of direction without prior bias. The median-rule-after-layer 6 control also retains the learned entrance and all learned b values; success of that hybrid would not establish that the entire learning process is unnecessary.

Primary outcomes

Probe reach is the fraction of the 24 injected identities found in any layer 130 output. Mixed true/probe outputs count as contamination. Strict true recall requires one pure output for each expected true identity/lane pair, with no additional mixed or duplicate occurrence. Full true-word success additionally requires order on all 12 output lanes. Pure false outputs interspersed between correctly recovered true pulses are scored separately.

Mixed outputs are not automatically missing true signals

The primary endpoints deliberately use a strict purity criterion. To distinguish contamination from extra false emissions, the complementary view counts probes that produce an output with no true identity. An inclusive true-recall diagnostic accepts a mixed output carrying exactly one true identity if that true identity appears exactly once; merged multiple true identities and duplicates still fail. These diagnostics are computed from the same raw outputs and are not substitutes chosen after changing the simulations.

Injection protocol and its scope

The true source is on lane0 and must reach all 12 final lanes. Twenty-four probes are inserted in source gaps3,8,…,118. Lateral and weakened central probes enter a single port at layer 7, visiting each lane twice; lateral tests visit both sides. Their times are the source-gap midpoint plus3, with the declared link jitter. Their base amplitudes are50% or100%, multiplied by the same paired lognormal link field.

Noise is a sparse pulse process: uniformly selected layer 7 lane and port, base amplitude uniform[0.3,1], and a bounded random timing offset. Exactly the same pulse realization is used with and without the true source. This does not test every noise process. Wrong-lane probes instead enter external source lane6; they test learned entrance geometry, not an interior route that was never active.

The source geometry matters. A homogeneous rule with central gain above the isolated-pulse threshold and lateral gain below it may preserve a single lane but fail the required broadcast. The learned-entrance median-rule control tests whether a simple interior rule suffices once that entrance problem is handled.

The physical kernel is inherited from the independently checked energy campaign. The extension carries passive provenance sets; tags do not change the charge, gain or recovery equations. Local tests cover analytic isolated-pulse thresholds, mixed provenance, true recovery and duplicate contamination. Clean learned and uniform4 full-network events and work exactly match earlier outputs. Frozen parameters, auxiliary section masses, fast drain, pump balance and monotone downstream provenance counts are audited across the campaign.

Provenance is the union of identities on currently active raw inputs at firing. It preserves mixed labels but is not a full counterfactual attribution of subthreshold charge memory. Strict purity loss should therefore be read alongside inclusive true delivery, false-only output rates, mixed-event counts and interval distortion. Per-layer profiles retain aggregate probe-reach rates, rather than per-identity disappearance histories.

These are numerical point simulations, not an interval proof. The nonoverlapping-auxiliary domain is enforced: solver failures are not dropped or counted as successful filtering. E remains positively bounded; no depleted-reserve behavior is claimed. Bootstrap intervals resample network seeds, not individual lanes or probes. Zero observed events does not establish zero risk in other realizations.
