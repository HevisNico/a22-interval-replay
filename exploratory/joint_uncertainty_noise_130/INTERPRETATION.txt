A22 — JOINT UNCERTAINTY AND NOISE: WHAT THIS PILOT SHOWS

The combination has now been simulated directly. Six new heterogeneous physical
realizations with relative uncertainty epsilon=1e-4 were acquired and tested with
low-level link noise. Every uncertain/noisy realization transmitted all 128 true
source impulses exactly once, in order, on all 12 lanes at layer 130 in the
no-decoy test. All six corresponding acquisitions passed the terminal training
criterion. Every scheduled outcome is retained; simulator failures: zero.

The nominal noisy regime also passes 6/6 baseline words. Its average relative
interval RMS is 0.518236%, versus 0.497467% with physical uncertainty and noise.
This small difference must not be advertised as an improvement: only six paired
seeds and six independent physical draws were used, with no inferential test.
The useful finding is coexistence in this tested regime, not a claim that physical
uncertainty improves timing or is harmless everywhere in the parameter box.

With 24 lateral full-amplitude decoys, both noisy regimes have 3/6 complete words.
Uncertain/noisy once-only coverage is 99.967448%, with mean 8.833 added timing
events and 0.500 removals across the 12 lanes. Nominal/noisy coverage is 99.934896%,
with mean 8.500 additions and 1.500 removals. High per-pulse coverage is compatible
with imperfect complete words because one extra or missing event breaks a word.
The joint test does not remove the already identified selectivity limit.

Both quiet regimes pass 6/6 baseline words and 6/6 mixed words. Quiet nominal
networks are identical across seeds, so this is one deterministic nominal quiet
reference replicated for pairing, not six independent successful devices.

The physical draw was made once for each cell/port/parameter and reused during
acquisition and test and in both uncertain regimes. Paired nominal/uncertain
conditions use the same latent link-noise keys. Acquisition and test keys are
disjoint. Four predeclared criteria were reported: transmission, complete words,
extra outputs and timing error. All failures would be kept separately from
simulator refusals; none was silently removed or replaced.

A defensible wording is:
"In a six-seed exploratory pilot at epsilon=1e-4 and low link noise, all six
networks learned and then preserved the 128-pulse word on every output lane in
the absence of decoys. With lateral decoys, three of six complete words remained
perfect. These results provide numerical evidence for the joint operation of
fixed physical heterogeneity and temporally varying link noise in this regime."

This is not a proof for all physical parameters in the box or all noise sequences.
It does not include the separate initial-state uncertainty delta, larger noise,
all decoy classes, gate-off controls, hybrid comparisons or a global energy claim.
The Gaussian log-amplitude distribution is unbounded, so a deterministic guarantee
for every possible noise draw is outside this finite-path simulator's domain.
A mathematical joint guarantee would require a precisely bounded noise class
and a validated inclusion of all relevant hybrid trajectories for that class.
