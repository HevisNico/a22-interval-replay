Energy, learning and the READY gate

Matched interventions · 130 layers × 12 lanes · 20 seeds per noisy level · English reproducibility package

What was corrected. The previous frozen-gain comparison was overinterpreted as a direct gate test. This rerun separates those questions, measures acquisition on all twenty seeds, independently reconstructs the physical dynamics for local validation, and integrates pump input from its own rate. No physical cost term was changed to favor either outcome.

Direct gate intervention: the primary result

Both branches load exactly the same normally trained checkpoint and start the test with the same gains, b, relaxed q and E=100. Both can learn. The sole intervention removes the READY condition from gain learning in the gate-off branch. Recovery learning retains its original law in both. Later differences in b or downstream traffic are consequences of this intervention, not differences in starting conditions.

Energy law and reserve scope

dE/dt = 10(100 − E) − W. W includes admitted main current, main fast drain, constant slow-phase power 1.1, admitted auxiliary current, the three auxiliary drains, actual log-gain movement and actual b movement. The source specifies unit coefficients. Values are model work units, not calibrated joules.

Static large gains have no maintenance penalty under this law. Stronger input may close admission earlier. The cost of creating an externally prescribed uniform gain, the external pulse source and any unspecified hardware overhead are not invented or charged. Integrating the reserve does not by itself supply a microscopic energy model.

Every cell starts at E=100 and must pass a finite-path positive-bound check. Across this campaign the smallest continuous lower bound was 57.438902, and the smallest event-sampled reserve was 96.544281. The sampled minimum is not claimed to be the exact continuous minimum. These runs do not reach depletion, and the production solver still refuses paths whose bound cannot guarantee E>0. No E=0 behavior or resource-limited necessity is demonstrated.

After training, the long quiet interval relaxes q and E without changing gains or b. Total work is integrated through the completion of main and auxiliary responses. Pump input during the measured window is integrated separately; adding final replenishment restores E to its initial value and makes total pump supply equal total work. The raw pump-window total is not substituted for the work endpoint.
