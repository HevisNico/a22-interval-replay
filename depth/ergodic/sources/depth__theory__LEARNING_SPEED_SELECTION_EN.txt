A22 — DOES LEARNING SELECT A PREPARATION-INDEPENDENT PROPAGATION DELAY?
Targeted admissible-history study, local proofs and remaining obligation
22 October 2026

ANSWER AND EVIDENCE LEVEL

The decisive distinction is between changing a finite learned prefix and
changing the initial state of every fresh downstream cell. In the tested
finite-prefix preparations, IDENTICAL fresh nominal layers recover the same
progression delay. Different persistent delays occur when the initial gain
perturbation is maintained throughout the fresh bulk. Therefore the bulk
tests alone do not demonstrate transmission of two competing learned speeds.
All infinite-depth conclusions still require a finite training-entry proof.

There are three distinct results:

PROVED WITH DIRECTED INEQUALITY CHECKS: two authorized initial states, under
the actual 128-pulse source protocol, produce distinct persistent learned
head delays in cell (1,0). Thus local learning does not erase all duration
freedom. This alone does not decide asymptotic propagation speed.

EXPLORATORY FULL-HISTORY COMPUTATIONS: six explicit authorized preparations
were propagated through 192 fresh layers. No terminal gains were copied into
new cells. Two homogeneous bulk preparations give different progression delays;
two different 18-layer prefixes followed by identical nominal fresh cells give
the same progression delay, with a surviving constant phase offset. A mixed
bulk preparation with the real ring connections exhibits a common regional
progression delay. These are point diagnostics, not validated enclosures.

PROVED CONDITIONAL INFINITE-TAIL RESULT: a full-history entrance contract,
suggested by the diagnostics, implies that deeper fresh cells cease changing
their gains. Two homogeneous initial perturbations then have explicitly
different asymptotic delays. A connected mixed-lane tail has one common
asymptotic delay. Directed checks establish the tail inequalities, not the
claim that the full learning prefix reaches this contract.

Separately, the supplied 18-layer future certificate supports a local
pipeline theorem with source gaps >=7, replacing global rest between waves.
This improvement applies to its existing certified domain, not automatically
to fresh deeper cells.

1. PREPARATIONS AND WHAT WAS ACTUALLY RUN

The physical constants are nominal and identical in all runs. The source
is lane 0 only, at times 8k, k=0,...,127. Every fresh cell starts at time zero
with q=0.1, auxiliary charges 0.025 in READY, b=0.08, reserve E in [50,100],
no pending currents, and

    z_i(0) = log(g_i,nominal) + eta,
    (g_0,g_1,g_2)_nominal = (0.3,1.2,0.3).

The first four preparations are nominal eta=0; plus eta=+10^-5 everywhere; minus
eta=-10^-5 everywhere; and interface eta=+10^-5 in lanes 0..5, -10^-5 in
lanes 6..11. The chosen eta is constant across the three ports of a cell
and across depth in each lane. All are choices inside the prescribed initial
box, with the same physical parameters. Independence of allowed choices does
not require that a selected realization be random or spatially heterogeneous.
Two essential controls, plus_prefix18 and minus_prefix18, apply the shift
only in layers 1..18, then use identical nominal eta=0 in every fresh layer.
They test memory transmitted by the training history without continually
reintroducing a different initial gain in new cells.

The interface is present THROUGHOUT training, with every actual cross-region
connection retained. It is not assembled by splicing unrelated terminal
states or emission histories. Separately trained isolated regions followed
by rewiring would require a different admissible preparation protocol; that
operation was not silently introduced.

All emitted pulses from each preceding layer, including the entire irregular
training transient, feed the next fresh layer. Missed pulses are not inserted
back. Main currents, protection, auxiliary mark/error phases, capped gain
updates and b updates are evolved during training. Only E is eliminated by
the existing uniform resource bound, which keeps the power gate open.

Four future probes at 4096,4116,4136,4156 are appended to the source. The first
is an allowed time >=1116; this delay permits a clean finite-prefix diagnostic.
The deep prefix's last training output is around 1119, so it would have been
incorrect to assume every new layer was already trained and quiescent at 1116.

point_diagnostic.py is a separate point-arithmetic implementation using exact
nominal auxiliary offsets derived from the ODEs, closed-form flows and scalar
bisection. It imports no original engine or native extension. It uses a 1e-9
external-event grouping tolerance and does not cover all weak event orders.
No original code was modified and no certified A22 campaign was launched.

Before extension, the nominal diagnostic reproduced all 21,302 archived
training emissions on the original 18 layers, with no count mismatch and a
maximum date discrepancy of 1.978150976356119e-11. This is a regression check,
not an interval proof. Each 192-layer diagnostic took about 14–17 seconds.

2. WHAT THE HISTORY REVEALS

All six runs have 58 training emissions per lane at layer 64 and 54 at
layer 68. They then retain 54 per lane through layer 192. The loss does NOT
continue until all training pulses disappear.

From layer 69 onward, every fresh cell retains its initial gains and b=0.08
in these diagnostics. The incoming training word has become sufficiently
spaced that every pulse can pass without exposing an ERROR current to an
open principal gate. Marks also finish before the main firing. Training is
still arriving, but neither gain nor recovery adaptation is actuated.

At layer 68 the plus/minus histories have maximum cross-lane cohort widths
approximately 0.104192/0.104203, and minimum same-lane gaps approximately
15.586453/15.586441. These are comfortably inside the proposed homogeneous
tail contract: cohort diameter <=0.11 and same-lane gap >=15.5.

The mixed-interface history has maximum cohort width approximately 0.006964
at layer 128, inside a smaller <=0.01 contract used for its block-contraction
proof. Its minimum gap remains above 15.5. These memberships are POINT
observations until the prefix receives validated enclosures.

Effective future delays per layer, measured over layers 160..192:

  plus bulk          0.666556597073    0.666556601278     0.666556592868
  minus bulk         0.666589905699    0.666589909907     0.666589901491

These are finite-depth slopes, not limits estimated with an error theorem.
The plus/minus difference is about 3.33086e-5 per layer. At layer 192 their
mean future latencies differ by about 0.00543269. In contrast, the prefix-only
controls differ in mean latency by about 0.000320851 at both layers 128 and

In the mixed case, the mean latency difference between the two half-rings

Coordination need not mean equal arrival dates: a fixed spatial phase profile
is compatible with one common limiting progression rate.

All four probe translations in each of the six runs agree to displayed point precision in
every active cell. This observation is weaker than a theorem for every future
word; the latter requires the boundary fixed-latency contract identified below.

3. AN ACTUALLY ATTAINED LOCAL NON-SELECTION THEOREM

Consider cell (1,0), whose only driven port is the source's central port.
Use the authorized initial states above, with eta=+10^-5 or -10^-5 and all
physical constants nominal. Define a=log(4), kappa=1/2 and

    h(u)=a/(a-kappa) [exp(-kappa*u)-exp(-a*u)].

The pulse at time 0 cannot fire this cell: even at its maximum charge,
0.1+1.2 exp(eta) h(1)<1. Its complete ERROR phase occurs while READY and
adds exactly 0.6 to z. Thus g becomes 1.2 exp(eta+0.6), below cap 4.

At time 8 there remains charge from the failed first pulse. Relative to time
8 the head charge is exactly

    0.1 + [1.2 exp(eta) h(1) exp(-3.5)] exp(-u/2)
        + 1.2 exp(eta+0.6) h(u).

Directed bounds put its first crossing in u in [0.43,0.46]. The time-16 pulse
arrives during protection. Its mark raises b from 0.08 to 0.18, while its
ERROR is entirely protected. Integrating b during that mark places reopening
between 18.1869 and 18.2003, after ERROR ends at 17.25 and before time 24.

From time 24 onward, each training pulse fires from exact rest. Its mark
ends before firing, its ERROR is protected, and recovery finishes before
the next input eight units later. These strict inequalities give an induction
through all remaining training pulses. There are exactly 126 firings, at
source indices 1 and 3..127. The final values are

    g_terminal = 1.2 exp(eta+0.6), b_terminal=0.18.

The persistent learned head delays satisfy

    eta=-10^-5: u approximately 0.4515317747619872;
    eta=+10^-5: u approximately 0.4515164025882775.

Their strict separation is greater than 0.000015372173709679.
check_learning_and_pipeline.py verifies the necessary directed inequalities
and supplies exact enclosing endpoints. This is an admissible learning
example, not a manually imposed terminal gain. It proves local memory of
the initial preparation, but does not alone prove different depth speeds.

4. CONDITIONAL INFINITE HOMOGENEOUS TAIL

Assume an entrance section provides complete matched input cohorts t_j^p
on all 12 lanes, including the training history, with

    max_j t_j^p-min_j t_j^p <= W=0.11,
    t_j^(p+1)-t_j^p >= H=15.5.

All downstream cells are fresh authorized cells as in Section 1, with one
fixed homogeneous eta in [-10^-5,10^-5]. There is no replacement of their
initial states by learned states. The nominal synchronized delay u_eta solves

    0.1 + 1.8 exp(eta) h(u_eta) = 1.

If every parent date lies between l and l+W, monotonicity and translation
equivariance place the child's first firing between l+u_eta and l+W+u_eta.
Consequently the head ages lie between u_eta-W and u_eta+W. The checker proves
on this whole tube that marks finish before firing, the first head precedes
raw-pulse endings and ERROR onset, all ERROR phases and raw tails end during
protection, and reopening precedes the next cohort. The slow recovery uses
the unchanged b=0.08, with duration 1+log(2.5)/0.08. The recovery slack relative
to H is greater than 2.2697, despite this relatively slow recovery.

Therefore g and b never change; each cohort produces exactly one emission
per cell and the cell regenerates locally. Since all rows use the same gain
profile, the output cohort diameter is no larger than its input diameter.
If every input component of the next cohort is at least H later, monotonicity
and translation equivariance give the same separation for its outputs.
The complete history contract therefore propagates through every fresh layer.

For any one cohort with entrance vector t, its n-layer image is bounded by

    min(t)+n*u_eta <= T_eta^n(t)_j <= max(t)+n*u_eta.

Dividing by n proves a common asymptotic delay u_eta for every lane, without
needing to infer an average from a finite regression. The exact directed
root brackets give

    u_plus  = 0.6665565970727810...;
    u_zero  = 0.6665732508807828...;
    u_minus = 0.6665899056987104...;
    u_minus-u_plus > 0.000033308625929372.

Thus the two preparations have provably different infinite-tail delays IF
the full authorized learning prefixes enter the proposed history contract.
The finite entry is currently supported by the point computations only.

IDENTICAL-FRESH-CELL COROLLARY. If two different upstream preparations both
enter this contract and all subsequent fresh cells are nominal (eta=0),
their asymptotic delays are BOTH u_zero, regardless of their entrance phase
profiles or different earlier learning histories. The bounded min/max
inequality proves this directly. The prefix-only controls are candidates for
this corollary. Their matching duration does not establish contraction of
all learned variables: in the tail the gains simply never change. The
mechanism is input spacing plus local protection, followed by propagation
through the same fresh physical response map.

For future words, also require the entrance's exact translated-cohort property
t_j^p=s_p+d_j. Local regeneration and fixed gains then preserve that property
and all future intervals. The finite training cohorts need not themselves
be translations of one common profile: the tail proof preserves their order
and separation without claiming their individual intervals are unchanged.

5. CONDITIONAL CONNECTED INTERFACE THEOREM

Fix a depth-independent lane pattern eta_j, including the half-plus/half-minus
interface. All three gains of a cell have the same multiplier exp(eta_j).
The physical graph retains both cross-region connections around the ring.
Assume full entrance-history cohorts have diameter <=D=0.01 and gaps >=15.5.

Let Delta=u_minus-u_plus. One layer increases cohort diameter by at most Delta.
Thus six intermediate steps stay in diameter D+6*Delta<0.010200. On this tube
the same physical protection and recovery inequalities remain valid, so no
plastic actuation changes the initial gains or b.

The gain scale cancels in the timing Jacobian ratios. A uniform lower matrix
has the three nonzero row entries gamma*(1/6,2/3,1/6), where gamma is a directed
lower bound on h'(largest age)/h'(smallest age). Its six-fold product has a
row-overlap lower bound beta>0.05734. Therefore the six-layer head map contracts
differences modulo common translation by at most 1-beta.

The six-layer image of a synchronous vector has diameter <=6*Delta. Hence

    osc(T^6(x)) <= (1-beta)*D+6*Delta < D.

The checked closure slack exceeds 0.0003735. This is an invariant normalized
domain for the six-layer block. Contraction gives a unique normalized fixed
profile; continuity/Lipschitz control of the scalar advance gives an additive
eigenvalue and one limiting delay shared by every lane. Bounded intermediate
offsets pass this result from six-layer boundaries to all depths. The limiting
delay lies between u_plus and u_minus. It need not equal the arithmetic mean.

Consequently, once this tail contract is entered, the real cross-links prevent
two different regional asymptotic speeds, while allowing a fixed phase offset.
This is compatible with different speeds in two separate homogeneous
preparations. The two possibilities in the question are not mutually exclusive:
within-device coordination and across-preparation non-selection can coexist.

Entry for the mixed case is suggested at layer 128 by the point histories.
No validated statement that every allowed preparation reaches this domain is
made, and no artificial stitching of the plus and minus histories is used.

6. LOCAL PIPELINING — AN OBLIGATION DISCHARGED ON THE EXISTING DOMAIN

For any certified cell let d be its head latency, a_i its parent latencies,
P_max a uniform upper protected duration, and E_max the maximum auxiliary
cycle duration. A sufficient local gap condition is

    H > d+P_max-min_i a_i,
    H > max_i a_i+E_max-min_i a_i.

Also retain the already checked within-wave first-root, gain protection,
masked-port and raw-tail inequalities. The outgoing pulse ends before the
main recovery and is included explicitly in the checker.

Proof: if the current wave has source time s_p, the cell is back at exact
READY charge and every incoming auxiliary has completed before the earliest
arrival belonging to the next wave. Its influential gains remain fixed;
masked gains and b stay in their admitted ranges. Each wave therefore sees
the same head equations after a local regeneration. Induct over source pulses
within each cell and over the feedforward graph. Other waves may still be
travelling farther downstream; no argument requires global quiescence.

For all 191 active cells in the existing 18-layer certificate, the largest
required local gap is strictly below

    6.965293252185588.

Thus H=7 has a uniform strictly positive local slack greater than 0.0347067.
Starting in the certified terminal section Q at an allowed first source time,
the same fixed-latency and word-preservation conclusion extends to source
gaps >=7. The 25 inactive cells have no incoming active chain and remain silent.
This is a new local proof using the existing root/gain/recovery enclosures;
the old proof's global settling bound below 12.791 is no longer needed
between consecutive source pulses. There is no assertion that the entire
network returns to Q between such overlapping waves.

For the conditional infinite fresh tail the relevant bound is different:
H>=15.5 suffices because b remains 0.08. The original future gaps >=20 satisfy
it. Positive per-layer head ages bound the active causal cone at each finite
time; combined with the positive per-port gaps this excludes an infinite
downstream event cascade in finite time. Together with local regeneration,
this gives a consistent infinite-strip construction conditional on entry.

7. EXACTLY WHAT REMAINS TO BE CERTIFIED

The decisive missing link is FINITE, not an infinite simulation:

For plus and minus, validate the complete original training prefix through
layer 68, including all emitted and omitted pulses, and certify 54 matched
boundary emissions per lane, cohort diameter <=0.11, gaps >=15.5, and the
boundary's translated future-cohort contract. For the mixed preparation,
certify an analogous boundary at layer 128 with diameter <=0.01.

The existing manual learning proof and campaign cover 18 layers, not these
new prefixes. Nominal regression agreement, a hash of a diagnostic trace,
or decimal intervals placed around point values do not supply this link.
Neither do four identical future-probe translations establish every future
word. The tail proof must remain explicitly conditional until that finite
prefix and future-boundary verification is performed.

Validate the two prefix-only controls as well at layer 68. If these candidate
entries are validated, the homogeneous tail theorem proves common duration
for these distinct finite preparations with identical fresh bulk, AND proves
non-universality when fresh initial gain offsets persist throughout the bulk.
The mixed tail theorem settles regional coordination at the connected interface.
Neither conclusion quantifies over every admissible history, parameter field,
or finite preparation. No single universal learning-selection theorem has
been established by these examples.
If an entry check fails through interval overestimation, that is inconclusive;
an actual physical violation would reject that proposed witness.
