# Scientific review targets

The package supplies the arguments and assumptions. The following points
identify the main targets for independent review.

1. **Physical box.** Check that the perturbed constants are C,M,L_m,L_s,L_e
   and that independent gains are retained in the Jacobian lower bound.
   The principal response kernel h remains the A22 kernel.
2. **Derivative ratio.** Check concavity of log h' and the within-cell
   age difference ≤W. This sharper estimate closes D=0.11 despite
   heterogeneity.
3. **Variable blocks.** Check that the six maps can belong to different
   layers, that differentiation segments remain in the tube, and that
   the common lower matrix γP yields γ⁶P⁶.
4. **Hybrid consistency.** Check the strict separation of marks, firings,
   ERROR phases, pulse endings and recoveries. The constant-gain and
   constant-b trajectory must satisfy the original actuation equations,
   rather than disable them by convention.
5. **Complete fresh states.** Check the initial integral 8χ, absence of
   saturation, first-input time ≥20, first-crossing slope and summability
   of q(0)'s effect. This variant's closure margin is positive but small:
   >5.20·10⁻⁶.
6. **Subadditivity on a restricted domain.** Check the comparison between
   the original and shifted orbits, without assuming invariance of K_W
   from every arbitrary starting point of K_W.
7. **Ergodicity.** Check stationarity of the effective field law, linear
   integrability bounds for the maxima, passage to all lanes, and the
   probability-one event shared by all preparations. Ergodicity of θ⁶
   is not assumed.
8. **Scope.** Check that universal entrance, an unspecified particular law,
   a general numerical value of γ, isotropy and applications outside the
   model are not presented as established.

The relationship to production is preservation of its equations while
extending the tail assumptions. This is not an archive replay or a new
certification of the upstream prefix.
