4. PROOF: FIRST DOWNSTREAM LAYER

The model's outgoing pulse is canonical:
p_F(t)=a exp[-a(t-F)] on [F,F+1), a=log 4. An upstream cell's learned gains
are not an amplitude multiplier on its emitted pulse. They affect its firing
date. The gains multiplying the received currents belong to the RECEIVING
cell. Therefore the large learned gains in layer 18 do not invalidate a
fresh-cell test for the first precursor received at layer 19.

Write kappa=.5, r=.1 and
h(u)=a/(a-kappa)(exp(-kappa*u)-exp(-a*u)), 0<=u<=1.
The directed checker gives h(1)<.557666240 and h'(1)>0. The ratio
(a/kappa)exp[-(a-kappa)u] decreases on [0,1]; its value at 1 is >1,
so h is strictly increasing on the whole interval. After a raw pulse ends,
its positive READY contribution decreases exponentially. Its all-time
maximum contribution is therefore gain*h(1).
Moreover (a/kappa)^2 exp[-(a-kappa)u]>1 on that interval, so h''<0
and h'(u)>=h'(1). The derivative margins used in the root checks follow
after subtracting the possible derivative of the decaying initial error.

The largest gain sum at a layer-19 cell receiving only two of these
precursor pulses is 1.5 exp(.00009). Even allowing the full initial
principal error .00001, the maximum excess charge is

   q-r <= .00001 + 1.5 exp(.00009) h(1) < .836584648 < .9.

The one-side-port case has an even smaller bound. Thus lanes 1,2,10,11
cannot fire, and lanes with no input cannot fire. Lane 0 receives all three
pulses. For its first head, the numerical inequalities are

   .00001 + 1.8 exp(.00009) h(.6) < .9,
  -.00001 + 1.8 exp(-.00001) h(.8) > .9.

With precursor width W<.020748341, all three raw pulses remain active at
t_max+.8. The charge is strictly increasing between t_min+.6 and this
upper endpoint; a uniform positive derivative is checked. Thus lane 0 has
one first ascending threshold crossing between those endpoints.

Auxiliary timing is also checked with finite-series directed arithmetic,
including the first READY auxiliary perturbation after an arrival >=20:
  threshold delay d in [.01999,.02001];
  ERROR onset age in [1.0998820107,1.1001180109];
  ERROR ending age in [1.2498670122,1.2501330124].
The possible initial terminal ERROR tail finishes before .001. These bounds
retain independent port constants. Auxiliary threshold root signs and the
monotonicity needed to exclude an earlier auxiliary crossing are checked.

Every MARK of the precursor ends before lane 0's earliest possible firing.
Every ERROR starts after its latest possible firing and ends before its
earliest FAST ending. Lane 0 therefore receives all marks while READY and
all errors while protected: neither b nor any z_i changes there.

All other receiving cells never fire. Their marks occur while READY and
cannot change b. Their ERROR currents occur after all raw currents have
ended, while the principal remains READY, and are admitted in full. Each
such ERROR changes its port's log gain by

       8 integral(v_e dt) = 8(1/10-1/40) = .6.

port's perturbed L_e. Caps are inactive because even the largest resulting

F+1+log(2.5)/b0 <173.026482163<174.
It then stays at exact q=r in the absence of raw input. No second precursor
firing can occur. All auxiliary currents have ended much earlier. The
earliest later layer-18 input is after 175.3099439833.

The original uniform energy barrier E>=50 applies throughout; gain and

6. CONSEQUENCE FOR OBLIGATION B

The leading pulse support shrinks {11,0,1}->{0}->empty, uniformly, without
a layer campaign. But deleting that support from a downstream history is
not equivalent to deleting its physical past. Its gain multiplier is
exp(.6)=1.82211880039..., far outside the effective fresh offset box.
At t=174 several charges are also outside r+/-1e-5. Waiting erases neither
the positive gain increments nor their influence on later firings. The
recovery actuator happens to remain fresh in this particular precursor;
replacing it by .38 would be equally unjustified.

One may replace the precursor, for later analysis, by the explicit section
data above in the first two downstream layers, retaining their correlated
dates and initial parameters. Cells at layer 21 and deeper have received no
raw precursor and retain their original unforced initial evolution. This is
a finite-prefix reduction, not a conclusion that the remaining inputs to
layer 21 are complete or satisfy any diameter/gap requirement.

What is still missing is a theorem propagating the remaining 104 complete
layer-18 cohorts through this altered two-layer prefix, retaining every new
partial cohort, all gain/recovery changes, principal memory, and event
orders. It must reach a suitable small-width boundary (or prove a more
general bridge for the boundary actually reached). The positive lower
diameter bounds in section 2 show that invoking the existing .08 local
lemma immediately, even on local triplets, would be false.

This work does not establish any entry depth, either uniform over the
original box or depending on the realization. It does not disprove eventual
entry, and it does not turn the reported layer-19 lane-6 refusal into a
counterexample. Arbitrary later future words remain outside this lemma.
