A22 rebuild scope and scientific source

The author has explicitly retained the complete A22 physical realization. This
work changes its implementation infrastructure, not its equations, topology,
parameter box, arithmetic precision, source protocol or event semantics.

Source texts supplied by the author:
- The Reset Operator A Structural Constraint on Recursivity V3(5).docx
- The_Present_as_a_Reset_Interval_v13(2).docx
- MODELE_COMPLET_REFERENCE(1).md

This package does not review the papers or claim to prove their general
hypothesis. R supplies the structural constraint: a threshold crossing arms
a finite interval of reduced admission coupling while internal dynamics
restore a below-threshold state. The first paper explicitly distinguishes
admission sensitivity from dissipation and whole-cycle contraction.

The retained A22 realization implements this structure through its READY,
fast and slow phases, closed principal admission after firing, continuous
charge decay and return to r before admission reopens. Auxiliary channels,
delayed currents, gain history and resource bookkeeping remain those of A22.
Protection at the principal admission boundary is not a claim that all other
state variables or recovery timing are independent of all external inputs.

The exponential laws, coefficients, topology of 18 layers and 12 lanes,
plasticity, resource supply and pulse protocol are retained realization
choices. They are not claimed to be uniquely deduced from R. The author has
already made these choices and has asked that this work preserve them.

The source model itself distinguishes historical numerical claims from
qualification under later code revisions. Successful software tests do not
supply an independent proof of enclosure of every admissible trajectory.
All inherited mathematical assumptions and unresolved proof obligations
remain in scope; this is an implementation optimization, not a new physical
model or a formal verification of the original mathematical construction.

RESEARCH RELEASE ADDENDUM
The physical realization described above is retained, but the effective
computational kernel is new. Numerical abstraction, time identities, per-port
first-delay enclosures and persistence differ from the original prototype.
Original source-file equality is not a proof of effective semantic equality.
See AUDIT_EN.txt for the conservative relaxation and remaining proof scope.
